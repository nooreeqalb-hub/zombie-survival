 🧟  SURVIVAL

They are coming. You are the last one left. How long can you survive?

The Story
The city is infected. Everyone has turned. No help is coming. You have a gun, limited bullets, and a long, dark night full of screams. Your only mission is to SURVIVE.

### Features
- Endless Waves - Zombies get faster and stronger with every wave
- True Survival - If your health ends, it's game over
- Scavenge to Survive - Find ammo and medkits before it's too late
- Boss Zombies - A bigger threat appears every 5th wave
- High Score - Break your own record

### Controls
- W A S D - Move / Run
- Mouse - Aim
- Left Click - Shoot
- R - Reload

### Your Mission
1. Kill zombies
2. Collect ammo
3. Stay alive as long as you can

### Can You Survive 10 Waves?
99% of players die before Wave 5. Are you in the 1%?
