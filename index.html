About This App

Zomby Survival is an immersive, action-packed survival game where you play as a brave Human survivor in a world overrun by zombies.

Developed by independent developer Rosh Rehman, this game is designed to be enjoyable for everyone, including blind and visually impaired players with accessible controls and audio feedback.

Features: Accessible gameplay, smooth joystick controls, human vs zomby survival, and challenging levels. Can you survive the apocalypse?
